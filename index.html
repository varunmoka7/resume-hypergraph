Your resume as a graph

One web page that shows your jobs, studies and projects, and the skills that connect them, ready to put on your own site.

See an example

The example is varunmoka.com, the site this tool grew out of.

More examples, with made-up people: a student, a product manager, a researcher with 95 entries.

Make your own

The tool is free and open source. It runs inside the AI agent you already use, so your resume is never uploaded to this site. In Claude Code:

/plugin marketplace add varunmoka7/resume-hypergraph
/plugin install resume-graph@resume-hypergraph

Then say "turn my resume into a graph". Codex, Gemini CLI, Cursor and Claude on the web are covered in the instructions on GitHub.

Phone numbers, street addresses and dates of birth are left out of the result. Nothing is published: you get one plain HTML file and decide where it goes.
